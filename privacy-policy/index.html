Privacy Policy

Theme Park Shark App

Effective October 1, 2026. This policy covers the Theme Park Shark iPhone app ("the app"). The themeparkshark.com website has its own privacy policy.

Short version: I collect what the game needs to work, I never sell your data, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What the app collects

Your account

  • Sign in with Apple. Apple shares an account identifier and an email address (often a private relay address). These identify your account.
  • Your profile. The username you pick, your shark's look, friends, and game progress (coins, items, ride log, scores and leaderboard standings).

Location

  • Precise location while you use the app. The game uses it to show collectibles near you, to know which park you are in, and to know which ride line you are waiting in. Your position is sent to the game server to check that a find or a ride is real.
  • Background location, only if you turn it on. Background Ride Detection notices the rides you go on and keeps queue games in sync while your phone is locked. You can turn it off any time in iOS Settings.
  • I don't sell location data and I don't use it for advertising.

Purchases

  • Apple handles every payment. I never see your card or Apple ID password.
  • The server receives Apple's signed transaction details (product, date, transaction id) so it can deliver what you bought, keep VIP active, and restore purchases.

Device and diagnostics

  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • Crash reports and basic performance data (app version, device model, iOS version) to find and fix bugs. These are not used to identify you.
  • Simple gameplay events (for example, that a game was started or finished) to balance the game.

Camera and photos

  • The camera shows coins and pins in the world around you and takes a ride photo when a game asks for one. Photos stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.
  • The app only adds images to your Photos when you tap Save Image.

Optional rewarded ads

The app may offer optional ads you choose to watch for a reward. If it does, Google AdMob serves them as non-personalized ads, with no tracking across other apps and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Google may process your IP address and device information to deliver and measure the ad. See Google's privacy policy.

What I don't do

  • No selling or renting personal data.
  • No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No third-party advertising profiles.

Who processes data for the app

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase, push notifications).
  • The app's hosting providers (the game server, database and file storage in the United States).
  • Sentry (crash reports) and Expo (app updates).
  • Google AdMob, only if you choose to watch an optional ad.

Other players

Your username, shark, level, scores and leaderboard spots are visible to other players. Your email and your location are never shown to other players.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete your account any time: open the app, go to Settings, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your shark, coins, collection, friends and progress. It can't be undone. (Deactivate is there too if you only want a break.)
  • Purchase records may be kept longer where tax or consumer law requires it.
  • To ask for a copy of your data, email contact@themeparkshark.com from the address on your account.

Kids

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email me and I'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to processing. Email me and I'll handle it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new effective date. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
