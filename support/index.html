Common questions

Do I have to be at a theme park to play?

No. At home you can find coins near you, play the mini-games and fill your collection. At a park, the rides, queue games and park coins light up.

Why does the app ask for my location?

The game is built around where you are: nearby coins, the park you're in, and the ride line you're waiting in. Background Ride Detection is optional and you can turn it off in iOS Settings.

I bought something and didn't get it.

Close the app fully and open it again. It checks for finished purchases on launch. For VIP, open the VIP screen and tap Restore. Still missing? Email me with the date and the item.

How do I cancel VIP?

VIP renews through Apple. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Theme Park Shark.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your shark, coins, collection, friends and progress. It can't be undone.

The flashing effects bother me.

Turn on Reduce Motion or Dim Flashing Lights in iOS Settings, Accessibility, Motion. The app swaps flashes and bursts for gentle fades.

Something looks wrong in a park (a ride, a wait time, a coin spot).

In the app, open Settings and use the bug report option, or email me with the park and the ride.
